Our 6 Program Pillars

Strategic Pathways to Self-Reliance

Shifting the narrative from charity dependence to structural community rights and administrative self-advocacy.

01

Katkari PVTG Empowerment

Securing constitutional identity, caste certificates, and institutional protection for Maharashtra's vulnerable indigenous groups.

Learn more → 02

Women’s Leadership & Livelihood

Capitalizing zero-interest revolving funds to dismantle predatory debt and build community-managed financial resilience.

Learn more → 03

Ankur Tribal Children’s Home

Residential education, holistic nutrition, and character-building for 55 children to break intergenerational wage-labour cycles.

Learn more → 04

Land Security & Agroforestry (FRA 2006)

Facilitating Individual Forest Rights (IFR), tenancy claims, and sustainable community forest management (CFRMC).

Learn more → 05

Community Health & Senior Care

Mobile diagnostic clinics across 30 hamlets, maternal wellness, and doorstep pension access for vulnerable elders.

Learn more → 06

Civil Identity & Legal Literacy

Training local Gram Sabhas to file formal administrative grievances and claim statutory rights independently.
